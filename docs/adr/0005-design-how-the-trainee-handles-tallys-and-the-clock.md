# Design how the Trainee handles Tallys and the clock

The single Trainee plays every Atemschutzträger and also controls time. The controls were decided with a throwaway prototype ([branch `prototype/trainee-controls`](https://github.com/lharzenetter/checkbox-simulator/tree/prototype/trainee-controls/prototype/trainee-controls)).

- **A Tally is tapped, never left on Check.** Each tap is one event for the device core. There is no placed/removed pair and no time spent on Check.
- **The world panel next to the device shows the Roster Tallys as key fobs**, drawn after the Tally pictured in manual §3.3 and grouped on one card per vehicle. Each fob carries a read-only hint of its Trupp, shown in a caption and in the fob's look: plain when not logged in, an amber outline while in an Anmeldefenster, and an accent ring once in a Trupp. Fobs never show an Alarm or the Einsatzstatus, so the Trainee learns to read the Checkbox ([Decide the contents of the fixed Tally roster](0006-decide-the-contents-of-the-fixed-tally-roster.md)). A Tally is tapped in by **dragging** its fob onto Check (the drop is the tap) or by **clicking** the fob itself, with the number keys `1`–`8` as shortcuts. There is no separate tap button.
- **One at a time.** Each member of a Trupp is tapped in separately, as on the real device. There is no batch selection.
- **The device keys are physical only.** The front-panel keys use pointer events, and the keyboard maps `↑`/`a` to Up, `↓`/`b` to Down, `Enter`/`c` to OK, `Escape`/`d` to X and `Space`/`e` to Check (these are only keyboard bindings, listed in a "Tastenkürzel" dialog). Enter and Space stay with a focused control outside the front panel and the Tally fobs, so the side controls remain usable from the keyboard. Neither the front-panel keys nor the fobs can take focus from Tab, and the keys not from a press either, because their keyboard bindings already reach them; the arrows and Escape always reach the device. The **Notausschaltung** needs X and Check really held together (`d` + `e`, or two fingers on a touchscreen), for ~4 s of **real** time at any speed. There is no on-screen helper and no modifier shortcut.
- **Clock:** pause plus the speed presets 1×, 2×, 5× and 10×. There is no skipping ahead and no "jump to the next Alarm": an Einsatz lasts at most about 30 min. On the keyboard, `→` and `←` step to the next preset up or down, stopping at 10× and 1× and doing nothing while paused, and `p` pauses and resumes.

Decided in [Design how the Trainee handles Transponders and the clock](https://github.com/lharzenetter/checkbox-simulator/issues/6) (renamed here to the device's wording, see [Reconcile the glossary with the LCD's on-screen wording](0003-reconcile-the-glossary-with-the-lcds-on-screen-wording.md)).

The Roster size and the Tally shortcuts were later changed by [Let the Trainee add and remove Fahrzeuge](0015-let-the-trainee-add-and-remove-fahrzeuge.md).
